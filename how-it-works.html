About the vials

2ml, Fresh, and Ready to Write

Every sample arrives in a 2ml plastic vial — enough to fill most standard converters at least once, giving you a real chance to test the ink in actual use rather than just a quick dab on paper.

Each vial is filled by hand from the source bottle on the day your order is packed. We don't store pre-filled vials. Your ink is fresh every time.
